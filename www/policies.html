Terms of Service

These terms describe how CraveCorner works.

What CraveCorner is

  • CraveCorner is a food-only marketplace that connects home cooks and small food sellers ("sellers") with nearby customers ("buyers"). Listings are for food made by the seller.
  • CraveCorner provides the listing, messaging, and discovery platform. It does not make, store, deliver, or inspect the food, and it is not a party to any transaction between a buyer and a seller.

If something goes wrong with the food

  • CraveCorner is only the listing page — it does not make the food and has never seen, tasted, or inspected it.
  • If food you receive makes you sick, causes an allergic reaction, or is not what you expected, that is strictly between you and the seller. Take it up with the seller directly — not with CraveCorner or the person who built the website.
  • For urgent health concerns, contact your local health department or seek medical care.

No in-app checkout

  • CraveCorner does not process payments, hold funds, or guarantee payment. When you place an order request, payment is arranged directly between you and the seller using the seller's stated payment methods.
  • CraveCorner never marks an order "paid." Any payment note on an order reflects only what a buyer or seller reported — it is not a payment confirmation.

Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years old to hold an account or sell on CraveCorner.
  • Sellers are solely responsible for complying with all applicable local, state, and federal laws — including cottage-food laws, permits, labeling requirements, and any required food-handler training. Rules vary by state; when in doubt, check with your local health department before listing.

Your content and conduct

  • List only food you actually make. Photos must show your own food — no stock photos, no AI-generated images presented as photos, no misrepresentation of ingredients, allergens, or prices.
  • You are responsible for what you post. Do not post anything unlawful, harassing, hateful, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Do not post anyone's private information — addresses, phone numbers, or other personal details. Listings show city and state only.

Enforcement

  • We may remove listings, posts, reviews, or other content that violates these policies, and we may warn, suspend, or remove accounts. Serious or repeated violations — including dishonest listings and food-safety deception — lead to suspension.
  • If we take action against your content or account, you may appeal through the in-app process. Appeals are reviewed by a human.

Changes

  • CraveCorner is under active development. Features and these terms may change; material changes to the terms will be announced in advance.

Privacy Policy

This policy describes what CraveCorner collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account: your email address (used for sign-in), and the display name, city, and state you choose to share. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to CraveCorner staff.
  • Seller listings. Shop and product details you publish — names, descriptions, prices, ingredients, allergens, photos, and your city/state and service area. Your exact address is private and is never shown to buyers.
  • Activity. Order requests, messages, reviews, favorites, and follows — the content needed for the marketplace to function.

What we do not collect

  • No payment details. CraveCorner has no checkout, so we never see your payment information.
  • No precise location. Listings show city and state only. We do not collect or display street addresses to buyers.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

On-device data

  • Some preferences (such as a dismissed demo banner) are stored in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us and are not synced anywhere.

Cookies and analytics

  • The pre-launch site uses no advertising trackers. If analytics are added later, they will be disclosed here before they collect anything.

Your control

  • You can edit your profile and listings at any time. You can hide your profile from public views in your settings.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your account data, contact us (contact details will be published at launch). Deletion removes your profile and listings; records we must keep for safety or legal reasons (such as moderation history) are retained as required by law.

Community Guidelines

CraveCorner is a food community. Keep it welcoming, honest, and on-topic.

  • Food only. Listings, posts, cravings, and messages must be about food — dishes, ingredients, kitchens, and local food culture. Non-food listings are removed.
  • Be kind. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks. Critique the dish, never the person.
  • Be honest. No fake reviews, no misleading photos or claims, no bait-and-switch pricing. Reviews may only be written by the buyer after a completed order.
  • No spam or scams. No repetitive promotion, no payment tricks, no phishing. If a deal looks wrong, report it.
  • Respect privacy. Never post someone else's address, phone number, or private details. Your own exact address stays private too — buyers see city and state.
  • Report problems. Use the report button on any listing, post, review, or message that breaks these guidelines. Reports go to a human moderator.

Seller Rules

Selling homemade food is a real responsibility. These rules apply from the moment seller onboarding opens, and breaking them can lead to listing removal or suspension.

  • Know your local law. You are responsible for complying with your state's cottage-food law, permits, labeling rules, and any required food-handler training. Rules vary by state — what is allowed in one state may be restricted in another. When in doubt, check with your local health department before listing.
  • Only list what your law allows. Do not list items your state or local rules prohibit home sellers from selling (this commonly includes certain perishable, meat, dairy, or alcohol-containing items depending on jurisdiction). If a product needs admin review — for example, perishable food offered with shipping — it stays in draft until reviewed. Do not work around the review.
  • List honestly. Accurate names, prices, serving sizes, ingredients, and allergens on every listing. If an ingredient changes, update the listing the same day.
  • Declare allergens every time. List known allergens on each product. Home kitchens carry cross-contact risk — say so plainly on your listings.
  • Your photos only. Every photo must show food you made yourself. Never present stock photos or AI-generated images as photos of your food.
  • Handle payments directly. CraveCorner never processes your payments. State your accepted payment methods clearly and honor the prices you quote.
  • Show up. Honor confirmed pickup and delivery times. If something goes wrong, communicate promptly — no-shows harm the whole community's trust.
  • Keep it clean and safe. Follow safe food-handling practices: proper cooking and holding temperatures, clean equipment and surfaces, and honest "best by" guidance on perishables.

Food Safety Disclosures

Food on CraveCorner is homemade — delicious, and different from restaurant food in ways you should understand before you order.

CraveCorner is only the listing page. We don't make, inspect, or deliver the food. If food you receive makes you sick or causes an allergic reaction, that's between you and the seller — take it up with them directly, not with the person who built this website.
  • Home kitchens vary. Sellers cook in home kitchens, which are generally not inspected like commercial restaurants. Standards, equipment, and practices differ from seller to seller.
  • CraveCorner does not inspect kitchens. When a shop shows a "declaration reviewed" note, it means a CraveCorner admin looked at the paperwork the seller submitted. It is not a health inspection, and it does not mean the seller complies with local law. Never treat it as a safety certification.
  • Allergens. Sellers are required to list known allergens, but home kitchens may have cross-contact with common allergens. If you have a severe allergy, ask the seller directly about their kitchen practices before ordering.
  • Rules vary by state. Cottage-food laws differ across states — what a seller may legally sell, and what labeling they must provide, depends on where they cook. Sellers are responsible for complying with their local cottage-food laws; CraveCorner does not verify compliance.
  • Use your judgment. Check listings carefully, read ingredient and allergen information, and message the seller with questions before committing.
  • When in doubt, skip it. If a listing looks incomplete or a seller cannot answer basic food-safety questions, choose another seller.
  • Report concerns. Use the report button on any listing that raises a food-safety concern. For urgent health concerns, contact your local health department.

This page is not legal or medical advice. Sellers are responsible for complying with their local cottage-food laws; when in doubt, check with the local health department.
