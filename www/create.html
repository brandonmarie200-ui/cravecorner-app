How seller onboarding works

Six short steps. No fees to list in the preview plan, and CraveCorner never takes a cut through in-app checkout — payment is always arranged directly between you and your customers.

  1. Shop identity

    Pick your shop name, write a short blurb, and upload a cover photo that shows off your food.

  2. Location

    Tell customers your city and service area. Street addresses are never shown publicly — city and state only.

  3. Fulfillment & hours

    Choose how customers get your food — pickup, delivery, shipping — and set the days and hours you take requests.

  4. Business info

    Add your contact details and any permits or cottage-food registrations your state requires. You are responsible for following your local food laws.

  5. First product

    List your first dish with photos, price, servings, ingredients, and allergens. Honest listings only — no stock photos of food you did not make.

  6. Preview & publish

    Review exactly how your shop looks to customers, then publish. You can edit anything, anytime.
